Diagnosis: previous attempts used mix-blend-mode: multiply (failed: needs opaque parent, killed visibility) and then opacity + filter (failed: opacity flattens contrast on black-bg source, smoke becomes muddy and invisible).

Correct approach: mix-blend-mode: screen. Screen blend treats pure black as transparent and lifts lighter pixels onto the layer below. White smoke on black source video becomes white smoke drifting on cream with the black bg fully invisible. This is the standard technique for compositing white-smoke stock footage onto a light page.

ALSO fix: in the latest preview the hero text is sitting too low on the page with a huge empty space above. Text should be vertically centered.

2c. Why screen blend works here:
    - Source video: white/grey smoke wisps on solid black background
    - Screen blend formula: result = 1 - (1 - bg) * (1 - video). Black pixels (0) leave bg unchanged; white pixels (1) push toward white
    - On cream bg: black areas of video disappear (cream shows through), white smoke lifts toward bright white drifting over cream
    - This is the bog-standard technique for compositing stock smoke footage onto light backgrounds — used by every premium agency site that does this

2d. The video container <div> needs NO bg colour. Removing the bg-[#FBF8F0] from the container that previous rounds added — screen blend works against whatever's behind, which in this case is the hero section's cream bg directly.
